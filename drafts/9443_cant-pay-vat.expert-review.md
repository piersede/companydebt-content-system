## Claims that need your verification / sign-off (YMYL, compliance-sensitive)
1. VAT late payment penalties: no penalty to day 15; **3%** of the day-15 balance at day 16;
   **6%** total by day 30 (3% + 3%); second penalty at **10%/year** daily from day 31.
   (Source: gov.uk/guidance/how-late-payment-penalties-work-if-you-pay-vat-late)
2. Late payment interest: **Bank of England base rate + 4%** from the first overdue day.
3. Time to Pay stops penalties accruing from the date requested if kept to.
4. Online self-serve VAT payment plan eligibility is stated CONDITIONALLY (returns filed,
   under self-serve limit, within deadline window, no other HMRC debts, cleared within max
   term). Exact thresholds are gated behind the live service - CONFIRM before hardening.
5. Statutory demand / winding-up petition company debt threshold: **GBP 750**.
6. HMRC secondary preferential creditor status for VAT since **1 December 2020**.
7. Personal liability framing: VAT is normally a company debt; PLN reserved for deliberate
   default/evasion; main director exposure is wrongful trading (IA 1986 s214). Confirm this
   is stated at the right strength (not over- or under-stated).
