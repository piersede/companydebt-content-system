# Commercial Value of Existing Content — companydebt.com

Growth-audit lens 2 (content value). Prepared 2026-07-10.

Data grounding: fresh GSC pulls (page-level, 12 months 2025-07-07 to 2026-07-06, plus last-quarter 2026-04-07 to 2026-07-06, URL-prefix property https://www.companydebt.com/), fresh Ahrefs top-pages pull (GB, 2026-07-01, USD values shown as cents in the raw API; converted below), plus the foundation briefs (inventory, Ahrefs, GSC, business model, competitors, repo context). The earlier agents' full detail files were not present on disk in this session, so every number below is either from my own tool pulls (verified) or from the foundation brief (attributed). Live spot-checks via WebFetch where noted.

Frame: the site's only monetisation is IP-appointment lead generation (helpline 0800 074 6757, 4-field form, 30-Second Test) plus disclosed network referral fees. "Commercial value" of a page therefore = probability its visitor is a UK director in financial distress (or a solvent director closing via MVL) x its ability to route them to those conversion points. Total UK organic is only ~8,080 clicks/year (~22/day), so every page's contribution is measurable and nothing can hide in aggregate.

## 3. Pages attracting the wrong audience

- Celebrity/news set (§1): non-UK, non-director, no intent. Harley-Davidson alone burns 39,594 impressions of US motorcycling interest.
- /articles/covid-19-effects-on-cruise-industry/: cruise passengers/analysts, largely US.
- /advice/frozen-bank-account/: page content is right (verified UK-director-specific); the query catchment is global. GSC brief: Thailand ranks via this page at 1.96% CTR. Consider making the title/H1 explicitly "UK company bank account" to shed generic international queries — impressions will fall, effective CTR and relevance signals should improve.
- /county-court-judgements/can-county-court-judgment-ccj-affect-getting-job/ (158 clicks) + /sample-letters/write-off-my-debt/ (149) + /sample-letters/request-a-reduced-monthly-payment/ (124) + /sample-letters/i-cannot-afford-to-repay-my-debt/ (25): predominantly personal-debt consumers. ~450 clicks/yr of distressed but off-market traffic (see §7 for the salvage).
- /insolvency/what-is-an-individual-voluntary-arrangement/: IVA is personal insolvency — a consumer topic on a corporate-insolvency site. 10 keywords in Ahrefs, "what is an iva uk" pos 21. Strategic misfit; either reframe as "IVA vs company options for sole traders/directors" or retire.
- /company-cash-flow-problems/biggest-struggles-for-small-business-owners/ (30,310 impr, 64 clicks, pos 31.7): "small business challenges" listicle audience — students, bloggers, US searchers. Ahrefs shows it as a top-15 traffic page with $0 value.
- Site-wide symptom (GSC brief): 37% of clicks are non-UK; USA = 669k impressions (15% of total) at 0.11% CTR. The wrong-audience problem is a measurable double-digit share of the site's whole search footprint.

Individually harmless, collectively an unmaintained 2015–2021 news morgue that dilutes the "current, expert practice" signal the YMYL stack works so hard to build. The disqualification/fraud pieces have residual value as enforcement-reality evidence IF folded into the director-conduct/liability cluster; the restaurant/biscuit items have none.
- /insolvency/insolvency-act-1986/ (55,698 impr, 53 clicks, 0.10% CTR, pos 14.2): a definitional page in the AI-Overview kill zone (GSC brief groups it with misfeasance 40k impr/0.01% CTR, /company-administration/ 13k/0.02%, business-recovery 15.7k/0.03%). These pages' remaining value is AEO citation presence, not clicks — worth keeping only because the AEO programme can use them; do not invest in CTR work here.
- /receivership-mean-business/ (80,137 impr, 47 clicks, pos 19.9): a malformed root slug ranking mid-page-2 for receivership terms that are (a) declining as a procedure and (b) split across three pages (§8). High impressions, near-zero strategic value in current form.
- Dated instructional oddities still in the sitemap (inventory brief): Paradise Papers (2017), Carillion supply-chain (2018, effect/affect slug), Heathrow-Covid, 124-pints. The 124-pints piece is one of the few pages with real backlinks (Ahrefs brief: deep links favour it) — keep the URL alive for equity, but it needs a redirect target or an update, not deletion.

## 5. Consolidation candidates

Ordered by expected commercial payoff:

1. **Strike-off/dissolution trio** — /company-strike-off/, /company-strike-off-and-dissolution/, /liquidation/company-strike-off-and-dissolution/. Three near-identical topics, ~120k combined annual impressions, best position only 13.8 (and that from the /liquidation/ child in the last quarter). One canonical survivor (the /liquidation/ child is currently strongest), 301 the others, then extend to capture "first gazette notice for compulsory strike-off" (2,400/mo, a named RBR gap). This is the single cleanest consolidation on the site.
2. **Sector stack** — 25 legacy /sectors/ posts vs 8 root-level sector pages vs /sector-insolvency-hub/ vs /sector-specific-insolvency/, with 8 direct duplicates (e.g. /sectors/carehomes/ vs /care-home-insolvency/, /sectors/construction/ vs /construction-insolvency/). A 25-page Bernstein rewrite programme is underway (memory: sector-pages-rewrite). Strong recommendation: do NOT rewrite all 25. GSC shows no /sectors/ page in the top ~120 organic pages — the legacy layer earns almost nothing. Rewrite only sectors with live search demand + data-hub support (construction, hospitality/pubs, care homes, retail), 301 the rest into the 8 root pages, and retire the two redundant hub wrappers. This converts an expensive editorial programme into a smaller, higher-yield one.
3. **Cost cluster** — /liquidation/how-much-does-liquidation-cost/ (59,527 impr, 185 clicks, the site's #4 Ahrefs-value page at ~$338/mo) vs /closing-a-limited-company/costs-of-closing-a-company/ (20,099 impr, 47 clicks) vs /insolvency/fees-and-costs-of-an-insolvency-practitioner/ (6,720 impr, 25 clicks). Three cost pages split the highest-CPC intent on the site (liquidation cost CPC $10.34). Keep all three only if their queries genuinely partition (liquidation cost vs closure cost vs IP fees); currently GSC shows the liquidation-cost page ranking 10.9–14.7 on queries the closure-cost page also chases. At minimum, hard-differentiate titles and cross-link with exact-intent anchors; better, fold closure-costs into the closing hub as a section.
4. **Receivership trio** — /receivership-mean-business/ + /insolvency/lpa-receivership/ (19,189 impr, 38 clicks) + /company-administration/vs-administrative-receivership/. One well-structured "Receivership explained (administrative, fixed-charge/LPA, vs administration)" page under /insolvency/ would outrank all three and fix the broken root slug via 301.
5. **BBL hub 7→2** — /bounce-back-loan-support-hub/ still earns ~345 clicks/yr across 7 pages (verified sums: what-happens-if-i-default 70, dissolving-a-company-with-bounce-back-loan 76, directors-liability 58, can-i-lose-my-house 41, cant-pay-cbils 40, can-i-close-my-business 34, bounce-back-loan-fraud 26). Demand is structurally decaying (2020 loans) and RBR outranks CD on the key commercial query ("closing a limited company with bounce back loan": RBR #2 vs CD #8). Consolidate to two pages: "Can't repay your Bounce Back Loan" (default + liability + house) and "Closing a company with a Bounce Back Loan" (dissolve + close + fraud risk). Fewer, denser pages have a better shot at the surviving demand.
6. **Micro-duplicates** (inventory brief, low effort, hygiene): /hmrc/security-bonds/ vs /hmrc/security-bond-notices/; /liquidation/director-conduct-review/ vs /liquidation/directors-conduct-report-2/ (the "-2" is a WordPress collision slug); /liquidation/company-liquidation-what-is-the-value-of-your-intellectual-property/ (29 clicks) vs /liquidation/intellectual-property-and-trading-assets-in-liquidation/ (11 clicks last quarter) — two live IP-in-liquidation pages; /liquidation/liquidation-hub/ nested inside the /liquidation/ hub.
7. **Pre-pack pair** — /what-is-a-pre-pack-administration/ (41,914 impr, 35 clicks, pos 27.6) + child /advantages-and-disadvantages/ (8,866 impr, 24 clicks). The child's unqualified slug is an artefact; one page would concentrate the signal.

## 6. Removal / decay candidates

The 2026 blog cleanup killed 66 posts; what remains under /articles/ (42 post URLs in the sitemap) is still roughly half news-morgue. Recommended triage:

- **Remove (410 or 301 to nearest topical parent), no meaningful loss:** Rivington Biscuits, Boulestin, USC, Homebase CVA, Man Utd debt, Wayne Rooney, Jamie Oliver restructure, Orla Kiely, Bobby Davro, Harley-Davidson, Paradise Papers, Carillion, Heathrow-Covid, wine scammers, cruise industry. Combined ~1,500 clicks/yr of non-converting traffic; the offset is a cleaner site-quality profile for a YMYL domain that just lost 63–89% of its traffic. Check the redirect-coverage ledger first (Quick Redirects only, per house rules) and verify none carry meaningful backlinks before removal — Ahrefs brief flags 124-pints, veterans, climate and covid pieces as the linked ones; those four should be kept/redirected with care, not 410'd.
- **Keep but stop maintaining:** the disqualification/fraud enforcement stories — or better, mine them as citable enforcement examples inside the director-conduct cluster, then retire the standalones.
- **Reframe or retire:** /insolvency/what-is-an-individual-voluntary-arrangement/ (personal insolvency); /company-cash-flow-problems/why-debt-is-not-always-a-bad-thing-for-your-business/ (27 clicks, generic finance-blog content that undercuts the distress positioning); /company-cash-flow-problems/biggest-struggles-for-small-business-owners/ (listicle audience).
- **Slug corrections at next rewrite touch (301 via Quick Redirects, not standalone projects):** /insolvency/personally-liabilty-of-company-secretary/ (typo, but currently pos 9.2 with 2.1% CTR — do it when the page is next rebuilt, not before), /receivership-mean-business/ (fold into receivership consolidation), /articles/how-will-carillions-insolvency-effect-its-supply-chain/ (removal candidate anyway).
- **Housekeeping:** testimonial-sitemap.xml (1 URL, lastmod June 2022) — retire the CPT from the sitemap.

Not removal candidates despite appearances: the 61 /liquidation/ long-tail Q&A pages. Last-quarter GSC shows this tail is the healthiest part of the site — company-vehicles-and-equipment-in-liquidation earned 52 clicks in Q2 alone (vs 71 in 12 months — accelerating), plus list-of-liquidation-documents (17), how-to-challenge-a-liquidators-decisions-or-fees (16), paying-staff-but-not-hmrc-before-liquidation (14), ccj-when-going-insolvent (13), leases-and-contracts-in-liquidation (10), which-creditors-get-paid-first (10). These are exactly the mid-funnel, director-specific questions AI Overviews answer badly and competitors don't cover. This tail is quietly becoming the site's moat.

## 7. Repurposing opportunities (existing traffic → stronger commercial assets)

1. **take-money-out → MVL funnel.** /advice/how-to-legally-take-money-out-of-a-limited-company/ (294 clicks, pos 8.5) readers are solvent directors extracting value — the precise MVL customer, for the site's highest-margin published fee. Add an "extracting more than £25k / closing the company: when an MVL beats dividends" section with the existing £17,750 worked example and route to /liquidation/members-voluntary-liquidation/. This is the cheapest new-revenue lever found in this audit: the traffic already exists, at position 8.
2. **Sample letters → email capture.** ~450 clicks/yr of distressed (partly consumer) traffic across 9 /sample-letters/ pages, on a site with zero email nurture (business brief: the Stressed Directors Guide is ungated, no newsletter exists). Offer the letter pack as a download-by-email. Even off-market consumer-debt visitors become a referral/audience asset, and the genuinely corporate ones (cease-trading-template, 116 clicks) enter a nurture path. Requires the owner's judgement on gating tone.
3. **shareholders-liable + liability constellation → one funnel.** The site's #1 page plus losing-house (264), can-personal-assets-be-seized (131), personal-liability-spouses (189), unenforceable-personal-guarantee (81), personal-guarantee-insurance (179; 59 clicks last quarter and rising, pos 8.5) collectively hold ~1,750 clicks/yr of liability-anxious visitors. None of them currently behaves as a designed funnel. A consistent liability-assessment CTA ("do you have a personal guarantee? 30-Second Test") across the constellation converts the site's actual strongest asset class.
4. **cease-trading pair → decision hub.** /insolvency/cease-trading/ (60,980 impr, pos 8.7) + /sample-letters/cease-trading-template/ (116 clicks) sit at the exact moment a director decides between strike-off, CVL and walking away. Restructure the main page around "what to do next" with explicit routes into the (consolidated) strike-off page and CVL — currently it's a definitional page monetising a decisional moment.
5. **Construction article → sector/data asset.** /articles/common-causes-of-construction-insolvency/ (46 clicks) should be folded into the Bernstein-rewritten /construction-insolvency/ page with the specced construction data (insolvencies-by-sector page is already registered in PAGE_REGISTRY) — replicating the pub-closures pattern (117k impressions at pos 8.4) which is the proven repurposing template.
6. **Winding-up petition content → creditor product.** The business brief confirms creditors are an unserved segment and the petition cluster is written for recipient directors only. The existing 3 pages contain most of the raw material for a "recovering money from an insolvent company / issuing a petition" creditor page — a second revenue audience at near-zero research cost. (Deliberate strategic call needed: acting for creditors changes the site's positioning.)

## 8. Cannibalisation risks

- **CVL vs /liquidation/ hub (active, costly):** Ahrefs shows the hub holding "creditors voluntary liquidation"-family head terms at pos 12–15 while the dedicated /liquidation/creditors-voluntary-liquidation/ page sits at pos 30–39. Google is picking the wrong page and neither wins. The June 2026 liquidation rewrite improved the hub (pos 18.5 last quarter); the fix is intent partition — hub owns "company liquidation (process overview)", CVL page owns CVL terms — enforced by internal anchors and de-overlapped H2s. Same watch-item for MVL: /liquidation/the-difference-between-solvent-and-insolvent-company-liquidation/ (29,588 impr) and the hub's top keyword "solvent liquidation" (pos 12) both crowd /liquidation/members-voluntary-liquidation/.
- **Strike-off trio (active):** three pages, ~120k impressions, none above pos 13.8. Worst case on the site (§5.1).
- **Cost cluster (active):** §5.3.
- **Receivership trio (active):** §5.4.
- **Director-liability pair (probable):** /advice/are-directors-personally-liable-for-company-debts/ (34,045 impr, pos 21.6, recently refreshed) vs /advice/can-personal-assets-of-directors-be-seized-from-a-ltd-company/ (pos 9.0) vs /advice/losing-house-if-company-goes-bust/ (pos 9.1). The refreshed head page underperforms the two long-tail siblings; check query overlap in GSC before the next liability-page investment.
- **Sector duplicates (structural):** 8 direct legacy-vs-new duplicates (inventory brief) — resolved by §5.2.
- **Security-bonds pair, director-conduct pair, IP-in-liquidation pair (minor):** §5.6.

## 9. Maintenance burden vs value mismatches

- **Over-invested relative to yield:** the 25-page /sectors/ Bernstein rewrite (Opus drafting + humanise + gates + IP sign-off per page) against a legacy layer earning ~0 measurable clicks. Trim scope to demand-backed sectors (§5.2). Similarly, CTR/title work on AI-Overview-absorbed definitional pages (misfeasance, insolvency-act-1986, administration) is wasted motion — the GSC brief shows the clicks aren't there to recover.
- **Under-invested relative to yield:** the /liquidation/ long-tail Q&A set (§6) — the best clicks-per-maintenance-hour on the site and growing, with no programme attached to it; and /hmrc/time-to-pay-hmrc/ — an open SERP, 44k impressions, one page, no cluster around it (vs 7 pages still standing for decaying BBL demand).
- **Strategic but patient:** the /data/ hub. /uk-insolvency-statistics/ earns only 34 clicks/yr but sits at pos ~7.9–8.8 with strong CTR (6.8% on "insolvency statistics" last quarter, the only new query among recent top earners) and fuels the active outreach conductor. Its monthly-update burden is justified as a link/E-E-A-T engine, not a traffic play — measure it on referring domains earned, not clicks.
- **Zero-maintenance dead weight:** the news morgue (§6) — costs nothing to keep but its existence is not free on a YMYL domain post-decline; quality-signal cost exceeds its click value outside the linked pieces.

## 10. Priority actions (this lens only)

1. De-cannibalise the closure/liquidation money core: strike-off trio → 1 page; CVL/MVL intent partition vs the hub; cost-cluster differentiation. (High impact, medium effort.)
2. MVL funnel from /advice/how-to-legally-take-money-out-of-a-limited-company/. (High impact, low effort.)
3. Build the designed liability funnel across the existing top-traffic liability constellation. (High impact, low-medium effort.)
4. Cut the sector rewrite programme from 25 to ~8 demand-backed pages; 301 the rest. (Medium impact, negative effort — it saves work.)
5. Retire/redirect the celebrity-news morgue after backlink checks; consolidate BBL 7→2. (Medium impact, low effort.)
6. Expand where yield is proven: time-to-pay page upgrade for the open SERP; keep feeding the /liquidation/ long-tail Q&A engine; creditor-side petition page as a strategic decision. (Medium-high impact, medium effort.)
7. Gate the sample-letter pack by email to start the missing nurture asset. (Medium impact, low effort; owner call on tone.)
