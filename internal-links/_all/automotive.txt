<p>The UK automotive sector finds itself in a uniquely challenging period. Brexit, debt burdens, and the massive slump in demand caused by COVID-19&nbsp;are adding pressure to balance sheets throughout the industry.</p>



<p>A revolution is underway in terms of the vehicles of the future, with the death knell sounded for the internal combustion engine – sales of new vehicles will be phased out by 2030, with electric cars set to become increasingly mainstream.</p>



<p>Dealerships can be traditional showroom-based businesses, including both listed ventures with multiple outlets and small independents.
