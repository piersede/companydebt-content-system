<p>There may be a perception that professional services firms are more sheltered from insolvency than other sectors – however, a growing number of insolvencies would suggest they are far from immune to financial pressures.</p>



<p>Professional services companies include lawyers, accountants, architects, consultants, engineers, and surveyors. It also encompasses financial services firms, such as independent financial advisers, financial planners, and insurance brokers. Any firm that earns its income from knowledge-based services fits into this category.</p>



<p>Because this is such a diverse sector, insolvency can stem from many different causes.</p>
