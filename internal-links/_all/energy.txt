<p>The UK energy marketplace supports some 150,000 jobs and contributes over 0.8% of GDP.</p>



<p>Coronavirus has impacted the sector significantly, putting particular strain on oil and gas providers. Among those which have ceased trading this year were Green Network Energy, whose customers switched to EDF, while Simplicity Energy also failed, with its customers going to British Gas.&nbsp; Yorkshire Energy was another failure from December 2020, with customers moved to Scottish Power. Meanwhile, another regional provider, Portsmouth’s Victory Energy went into voluntary liquidation owing more than £131,480 to businesses.</p>
